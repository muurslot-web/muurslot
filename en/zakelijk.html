For insurers and bike dealers

An installed anchor costs less than a paid-out cargo bike.

We install throughout the Netherlands, at one fixed rate per installation. Your policies have no postcode boundary, and neither does our coverage. We already work with bike shops that sell the anchor with a new bike; the same advantage is available to insurers and underwriting agents.

One rate, all of the Netherlands

Your policyholders are everywhere. A regional provider forces you into a patchwork: yes in the Randstad, not in Zeeland, and a different rate per area. We install throughout the country for one price. That makes it predictable: you know what an installation costs, wherever the policy runs.

The sum

A paid-out electric cargo bike easily costs three to five thousand euros. An installed anchor with chain lock is a fraction of that. If it prevents one theft in fifteen installations, you break even. In the big cities that is no gamble.

The gap you have now

Your policy often requires a second lock to a fixed object. On the street that is a lamp post. At home hardly anyone has such a point, so nothing gets locked to anything. We create that point, at the customer’s home, throughout the Netherlands.

Provable from day one

With every installation we deliver a dated photo report: the anchor with a legible ART mark, the fixing and the chain through the anchor. At present it is practically impossible to establish afterwards whether a policyholder used a fixed object. With this report it is recorded on the day of installation — and that saves discussion at the moment there is a claim.

Nationwide coverage

We install from Groningen to Maastricht, at the same rate everywhere. No surcharge per kilometre, no postcodes left out, no separate arrangements per region. We combine trips and plan several installations on one day in the same part of the country.

How we work together

We already do it with bike shops: they put the anchor on the receipt at the sale, submit the job and keep their margin. With insurers it can be per installation at a fixed rate, or as a prevention package per policy that includes anchor, lock and installation.

They submit jobs to us

Also for other businesses

Besides bike dealers and insurers, scooter shops, motorbike dealers, cargo bike shops, caravan dealers and motorhome dealers can also come to us for an installed wall anchor, as can insurance offices, underwriting agents and bike leasing companies. The same goes for marinas, campsites, homeowners' associations, housing associations, day nurseries, schools, hospitality businesses, delivery services and care organisations with many vehicles in one place. If you only sell the anchor, for example in a shop or web shop, we offer the installation with it.

We are not insurance advisers and do not act as insurance intermediaries. We supply and install security, and invoice it as a business. Email info@muurslot.com and we will send our rates.
