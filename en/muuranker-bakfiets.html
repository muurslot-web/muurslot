Cargo bike and electric cargo bike

Wall anchor for your cargo bike

A cargo bike is rarely ridden away; it is too conspicuous for that. It is lifted by two people and put in a van, frame lock and all. What stops that is a connection to the building itself: an anchor in your outside wall or in the floor of your shed, with a heavy chain through it.

We fit an ART-4 wall anchor in the spot you point out. You don’t need to be home for it.

Locking your cargo bike to the wall

Take a 180 cm chain. That gets you around the box, around a real part of the frame and through the anchor. A 150 cm chain is often just too short in practice. We recommend an ART-4 chain, and feel free to take the heavy one: this chain stays on your wall and doesn’t need to go anywhere.

Does your cargo bike stand sometimes in front of the house and sometimes in the shed? Then in the same visit we fit a second anchor for €69.95, each with its own chain. What you have to go and fetch, you don’t do.

What insurers ask

For an electric cargo bike a separate second lock is almost always required, of at least ART-2. From an insured value of €2,500, for bikes up to three years old, Nh1816 requires you to lock it to a permanently fixed object. Kingpolis asks for a fixed object that cannot be moved. A tracker is required by ENRA from €3,500 and by Fietszeker always.

Bright reported on 26 February 2025 that ENRA no longer insures new Urban Arrow cargo bikes in twelve cities, including Amsterdam, The Hague, Leiden and Utrecht. A tracker only looks for your cargo bike once it is gone. An anchor makes sure it doesn’t get away.

With every installation you get a dated photo report with the ART mark legible in the picture. If you ever make a claim, you send it along. All requirements per insurer are under Insurance.

What it costs

Anchor and installation cost €99.95 in one go, or you rent from €3.19 a month with free installation. The same price everywhere in the Netherlands and no call-out charge.

For comparison, per year: insuring a €5,000 electric cargo bike against theft costs €403.20 in Amsterdam (Overstappen.nl, premiums from April 2026). A tracker with a Kiwa mark, such as the Tracefy Solo, costs about €125 and then €69.95 a year. You rent the anchor with a 180 cm ART-4 chain from us for €87.72 a year, which is €7.31 a month.
