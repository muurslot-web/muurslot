How it goes

You point out the spot. You don’t have to do anything else.

No tools, no ordering parts, no afternoon off work. Your chain lock is already on it when you get home. We are based in Sassenheim and drive all over the Netherlands, for the same price everywhere. We plan our trips so that we do several anchors in one day; that is why you don’t pay a call-out charge.

Watch an installation

Step 1

You decide where it goes

You tell us where the anchor should go — side wall by the shed, wall next to the front door, garage floor.

Take a photo of that spot and put a cross on it. In the order form you simply tap your own photo and a red cross appears. We burn that cross into the photo, so we know exactly where the anchor goes. No fuss with "left of the door, roughly at hip height".

Step 2

We check whether it can be done

From your photo we can usually already see what kind of brickwork it is and whether the anchor makes sense there. If we are unsure, we call you. If it cannot go where you want it, we tell you and the job does not go ahead. You then get your money back in full.

Step 3

We install, you don’t need to be home

Four 16 mm holes, and then whatever the wall needs: bolts where the brick is hard enough, chemical anchoring with threaded rod and injection mortar where it is soft, old or perforated. That costs you nothing extra. The plate is fixed with four bolts. In each bolt head we hammer a steel ball, so an Allen key no longer fits. The plastic cap goes over that. We clear up the debris, and you get a dated photo report to keep for your insurer.

Did you order a chain lock? When we leave, it is already through the anchor and locked. So it cannot go anywhere while you are out. All keys and the key card go through your letterbox in a sealed envelope.
