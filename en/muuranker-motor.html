Motorbike, scooter and moped

Wall anchor or ground anchor for your motorbike or scooter

Two people can easily lift a scooter into a van. A disc lock stops it on its own wheels, but not when it is lifted. Only when the chain runs through an anchored point does a thief have to take the wall or the floor with it.

We fit an ART-4 anchor in the wall or in a concrete floor. In the floor it is called a ground anchor or floor anchor. For a motorbike we prefer to put it in the floor of your garage or shed rather than in the outside wall: there a thief can get at it with both hands and has room to lever. A concrete driveway works too.

What insurers ask

For a motorbike, some insurers simply make an anchor a condition. If your motorbike is parked outside, InShared requires a chain or U-lock of at least ART-4 to an object anchored in the ground. Univé recommends a wall or floor anchor. For expensive motorbikes ART-5 is often required, and often an immobiliser with SCM class 1 as well.

For a scooter or moped ART-3 is the minimum, but for newer scooters insurers regularly ask for ART-4. InShared charges a €400 excess instead of €200 if your scooter was not locked to an object anchored in the ground. All requirements per vehicle are under Insurance.

Which chain goes with it

For a motorbike we recommend a 200 cm ART-5 chain, the heaviest class there is. A chain like that easily weighs six kilos, so you leave it at home by the anchor. For a scooter or moped we recommend a 150 cm ART-4 chain. The anchor itself is always ART-4. Read more about the anchor and the locks under The anchor.
