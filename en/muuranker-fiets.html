Bike, e-bike and road bike

Wall anchor for your bike or e-bike

At home a bike is often locked least securely. The lock goes around the rear wheel, a garden gate or a drainpipe, and that does not stop a thief. A wall anchor gives you a fixed point that doesn’t go anywhere. We drill an ART-4 anchor into your outside wall, the wall of your shed or the floor, and your chain lock goes through it.

You don’t need to be home for it. You point out the spot with a photo and a cross, we come by and fit the anchor. If you order a chain from us, it is already through the anchor when we leave and the keys go through your letterbox.

What your insurer asks

For an e-bike a separate second lock is almost always required: ENRA, ANWB and Univé always ask for it. For an ordinary bike ENRA and Univé ask for it from €3,500. A plug-in chain that you click into your frame lock does not count. It must be a separate lock with its own key.

Some insurers also want your bike to be locked to a fixed object. Kingpolis and Fietszeker ask for a fixed object for an ordinary bike, such as a lamp post or bike rack. For an e-bike Kingpolis requires a fixed object that cannot be moved. At home you usually don’t have one. Also check your home contents insurance: a bike stolen from your garden or driveway is often not covered by it.

All requirements per insurer and per vehicle are listed under Insurance.

Which lock goes with it

The anchor is always ART-4. The lock determines your class. For an ordinary bike ART-2 is the minimum the ART Foundation recommends. We recommend ART-3, a 120 cm chain lock, and if your bike stands outside at night in the city, go one class higher. For an e-bike most insurers now ask for ART-3, and we recommend a 150 cm ART-4 chain.

At home weight doesn’t matter: this chain hangs on the anchor and doesn’t go anywhere. Road bikes often have no lock either. If it leans against the wall for a moment after a ride, a chain already hanging on the anchor is on in five seconds. Read more about the locks under Locks.

Where the anchor goes

On your outside wall, in the wall or floor of your shed or storage room, or in a shared bike shed if the owner agrees. Brick, concrete and sand-lime brick are fine. In soft or old brick we anchor chemically, with injection mortar, and that costs you nothing extra. We decide on site what is needed.

What it costs

Anchor and installation cost €99.95 in one go, or you rent from €3.19 a month with free installation. The same price everywhere in the Netherlands and no call-out charge.

For comparison, per year: insuring a €2,750 e-bike against theft costs €208.92 in Amsterdam (Overstappen.nl, premiums from April 2026). A tracker with a Kiwa mark, such as the Tracefy Solo, costs about €125 and then €69.95 a year. You rent the anchor with a 150 cm ART-4 chain from us for €83.88 a year, which is €6.99 a month.
