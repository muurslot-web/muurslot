Before you book

Nine things you want to know.

Is a bike stolen from my garden covered by my home contents insurance?

Often not. Theft from the garden or driveway is excluded by many home contents policies, and then you only have bike insurance. That almost always requires a second lock, and more and more often that your bike is locked to something that cannot be moved. Check your policy; it is usually under theft outside the home.

My cargo bike can no longer be insured. What now?

Then prevention is all that is left. With an anchor in your wall and a heavy chain through it, your cargo bike will not be lifted into a van. The photo report you get at the installation shows later that your security was in order, should you take out insurance again after all.

I rent my home. Is this allowed?

That is up to your landlord or homeowners' association, not us. Ask in writing — an email is enough, and then you have it in black and white. In practice most landlords agree, because a wall anchor is a small intervention and it prevents damage and nuisance. If the answer is no, you can suggest placing it at the bike shed or applying together with the neighbours. We install where you tell us and do not check the permission.

What if I move house?

If you rent the anchor from us, the rental can pass to the new occupant. If you bought it, it can be removed. That is not a matter of simply unscrewing it — the bolt heads are sealed with a steel ball, so it takes a drill or angle grinder — but it can be done. What remains are four holes in the wall, which you can fill. If you rent your home, ask your landlord whether it can stay; often the next occupant simply wants to keep it.

I don’t have my own outside wall

Then more is often possible than you think. An anchor can also go into the floor or wall of your own storage room, in a shared bike shed or in the entrance hall — provided the owner agrees. Concrete and sand-lime brick are fine surfaces. Send a photo of the spot you have in mind and we will tell you in advance whether it can be done.

I don’t live in the Randstad

It doesn’t matter. We install throughout the Netherlands for the same price, without a call-out charge. We can do that because we combine our trips: we plan several anchors on one day in the same part of the country. If you live far away, it may therefore take a little longer before we come by — you will hear in advance which day it will be.

Who comes round?

We do. Muurslot does not outsource anything and does not work with subcontractors or intermediaries — the person you speak to on the phone is the one who drills into your wall. We are based in Sassenheim and drive all over the Netherlands. You will hear in advance which day we are coming.

What if there is a pipe or cable in that wall?

You know your own house, we don’t. If you know that water, gas or wiring runs through that wall, tell us — then we keep well away or choose another spot together. Not sure? Then choose a spot where definitely nothing runs: low down near the ground, away from sockets, switches, taps, the meter cupboard and the boiler. Pipes and cables usually run vertically or horizontally from such a point. If the surface turns out to be unsuitable on site, we do not drill and you get your money back. If, despite everything, something goes wrong that is down to us, we will put it right.

What does it cost to have a wall anchor installed?

€99.95 in one go, including VAT. For that you get the ART-4 anchor, the fixing your wall needs, the drilling and the travel. We do not charge an hourly rate, and chemical anchoring costs nothing extra. A second anchor in the same visit costs €69.95. Rather not pay a large sum at once? Then rent the anchor from €3.19 a month, with free installation and a minimum term of 36 months.
