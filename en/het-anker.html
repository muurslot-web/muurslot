What goes on your wall

Wall anchor, ART-4 approved.

ART-4 is the class insurers usually accept for a motorbike. For a bike that is generously over-dimensioned, and that is exactly the point: the anchor goes into the wall once and should never be the problem after that. If you buy a heavier lock later, it simply fits.

The anchor is as strong as the wall around it. An expansion bolt pushes from the inside against the wall of the drill hole, and in soft or old brick that pressure can split the brick — especially close to an edge. On a wall like that we anchor chemically: threaded rod in injection mortar, which bonds along the whole length of the hole instead of at a few pressure points. More work, but you pay nothing extra for it. We decide on site what is needed.

The same anchor can also go into a concrete floor, for example in your garage, your shed or on a concrete driveway. Then it is called a ground anchor or floor anchor. For a motorbike the floor of the garage or shed is usually the best place; more about that under wall anchor or ground anchor for your motorbike or scooter.

What you hang on it

The lock determines your class, not the anchor.

This is your home chain: it hangs on the anchor and doesn’t need to go anywhere. So take the heaviest that fits — weight is no objection here, because you don’t carry it around. If you order one from us, it is already through the anchor when we leave and the keys go through your letterbox. Want to use your own chain? Fine, then we only book the installation.
