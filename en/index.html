Annual check

Once a year we come by. We check whether the anchor is still tight, whether the brickwork around it has not cracked and whether your lock still runs smoothly. You get a dated photo report — proof for your insurer that your security was in order when it mattered.

This is completely separate from the purchase. You can switch it on later, and cancel it any month.
